How this template works

Each month has one theme, and every channel feeds that month's in-practice workshop and offer. Social media attracts, email engages, the workshop converts, and the offer amplifies through referrals. Themes and topics below are starting points: swap any of them and the structure still holds.

Delight sits at the centre: fast replies to DMs and forms, calling every workshop sign-up, and celebrating patient wins.

Channel Flywheel stage Job this quarter
Social media (Instagram + Facebook) Attract Post Monday to Friday; 80–90% value, 10–20% invitation
Email + text Engage One weekly newsletter plus a short sequence for each workshop and offer
Internal workshop Convert One evening talk per month in the clinic, closed with a clear next step
Internal offer / promotion Amplify One seasonal referral or reactivation campaign per month, plus an always-on friends-and-family voucher

Monthly planning meeting: hold it in the last week of the previous month. Fix the theme, workshop date, offer, testimonial patients and weekly topics, then batch content so you stay 2–4 weeks ahead.

Font: Wotfard. Semibold or Black for headlines, Regular for body.

Logo story to reuse: the mark re-orders "evolve" into "we love" and points upward, like patients climbing the health-score scale. Use the animation at the start of reels and in the December Gift of Health content.

The 3I content mix (weekly):

  • Inspirational: patient stories, testimonials, your own why, team moments
  • Informational: simple "how it works" reels, carousels, blogs and longer videos
  • Invitational: workshop invites, offers, "DM us" prompts; keep these to 10–20% of posts

Voice: practical, warm and 10-second simple. Avoid chiro jargon, anti-medicine messaging and back-pain-only framing.

UK compliance check: before posting, run health claims past the ASA/CAP guidance for chiropractors and the GCC Code, especially testimonials and anything about children. Send marketing emails only to people who have opted in (UK GDPR/PECR), and publish simple terms for every offer and prize draw.

Workshop

Title: Your Nervous System 101: why you feel the way you feel. When: Thu 22 Oct, 7pm, in clinic (45 minutes plus Q&A).

  1. Opening: Dr Andrew's personal story and why Evolve exists
  2. Relatability + authority: the spine and nervous system explained simply, with a model and the "we love" logo story
  3. Case studies: 3–5 real patient journeys (with written consent)
  4. Call to action: book a new patient exam that evening at a workshop-only rate, or a free follow-up call

Promote with: clinic flyers from 1 Oct, a booking page with a short video invite, stories countdown from 8 Oct, and flyers with two community partners (for example a gym, yoga studio or café).

Offer: Spine Check for a Friend

From World Spine Day (16 Oct) to 15 Nov, patients refer a friend or family member, who gets a reduced new patient exam. Every referrer enters a prize draw (for example a local restaurant voucher). Brief the front desk to mention it at every checkout, and give each patient a printed referral card.

Instagram grid pattern

A repeating colour rhythm makes the grid look planned, in the style of the PWC Chiropractic profile you referenced. Instagram's profile grid shows three posts per row, so rotate three tile styles and the month's accent colour will band across the rows.

Grid position Tile style Background Typical post
Left Patient story / quote tile Grass with white text, Leaf quote marks Monday testimonial
Middle Real photo or reel cover Natural photo, small logo mark Tuesday reel, Wednesday BTS
Right Carousel or invite cover Smoothie, or the month's accent (Teal / Sky / Ocean) Thursday carousel, Friday invite

Canva set-up: build one master template per tile style with the Evolve colours and Wotfard, then duplicate it for each post. Reel covers should use the same headline position so the grid stays tidy.

Pinned posts: pin (1) Dr Andrew's "why" reel, (2) "What to expect at your first visit", and (3) the current month's workshop or offer.
